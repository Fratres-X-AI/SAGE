# Commercial licensing

SAGE is licensed under **[FSL-1.1-ALv2](LICENSE)** (Functional Source License).

## What that means

| Allowed without buying a commercial license | Not allowed without a commercial license |
|---------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Internal production use | Offering SAGE (or substantially similar forensics/custody) **as a product/service to others** |
| Self-host for your company | Competing SaaS / managed “incident pack” platform built on SAGE |
| Non-commercial education & research | Reselling SAGE as a substitute product |
| Professional services for a licensee | |

After **two years** from each release’s availability date, that version automatically becomes available under **Apache-2.0** (see LICENSE — Grant of Future License).

## Buy a commercial license

If you want to offer competing products/services, white-label, or need terms beyond FSL, contact **Fratres-X-AI** for a commercial license.

Open a GitHub issue titled `Commercial license` or email via the org contact on [github.com/Fratres-X-AI](https://github.com/Fratres-X-AI).

## Note on prior Apache-2.0 snapshots

Commits published under Apache-2.0 **before** the FSL change remain usable under Apache-2.0 for those snapshots. **Current `main` and releases from this change forward** are FSL-1.1-ALv2.
